Merit Badge Workbook prints requirement checklists, note-taking workbooks and
blue cards for Scouting America merit badges.

Please read this before you hand anything it prints to a Scout.

THE REQUIREMENTS ARE A SNAPSHOT
This app ships a copy of every badge's requirements, taken on the date shown
on the Options tab and printed on every sheet it generates. Requirements
change. Use Requirements > Check for updates before each new class rather
than trusting the built-in copy indefinitely.

THE OFFICIAL REQUIREMENTS ARE NOT THESE
The requirement text belongs to Scouting America. These sheets are a
note-taking aid for Scouts and counselors. The official, current requirements
are the ones at scouting.org and in the current merit badge pamphlet. Do not
pass the generated files off as official workbooks.

BLUE CARDS ARE UNOFFICIAL
The card this app draws is marked unofficial on every panel, and a council's
fillable form filled in by this app is still a printout. Some councils accept
printed cards and some require the pre-printed stock from the Scout shop.
Check with yours before a Scout carries one to a board of review.
